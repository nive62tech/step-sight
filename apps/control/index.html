Keep Desktop Guide open and phone control enabled on your PC.

  1. Open Desktop Guide on Windows and sign in.
  2. In Preferences → Control from your phone, optionally allow Autonomous tasks, then select Enable phone control.
  3. Sign in here with the same account. Your computer will appear automatically.

Phone control lasts one hour. The PC must stay awake and unlocked. Local Stop disconnects the phone. Closing this web page does not stop a running task.

Prompts use the app’s browser launcher. Guided mode leaves clicks to you on the PC; Autonomous mode handles ordinary actions. Login, CAPTCHA and final commitments still need you.
