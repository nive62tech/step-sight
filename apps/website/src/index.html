A few things to know.

What is Desktop Guide?+

Desktop Guide is an AI companion for Windows that understands a task from your prompt and uses live screenshots of your permitted window to suggest the next step. It can open a browser for your goal, point to controls, check the result, and optionally click, scroll and type with local consent. It is an unsigned development pilot.

Does it work across every app automatically?+

The pilot works within one selected or opened window. It can launch a browser, but you must open and select native apps yourself. Switching apps, moving a link through the clipboard, file uploads and unsupported actions need your help. It pauses when the screen or next step is uncertain. Full workflows across real applications are still being qualified.

Can it book a Tatkal ticket or send a WhatsApp message?+

These are examples of tasks it could help you work through, not promises of completed integrations or guaranteed outcomes. It can guide ordinary navigation and form steps; you handle login, CAPTCHA, payment, booking confirmation and pressing Send. Tatkal availability and booking success are not guaranteed. The website’s meeting, WhatsApp, train and calendar walkthroughs are simulations. Signed-in Google Meet, IRCTC and WhatsApp workflows have not been qualified end to end.

What do I need to try it?+

You need Windows 11 x64, an invited pilot account and internet access. Download the ZIP, extract it to a writable folder and open DesktopGuide.App.exe. The .NET runtime is included. The app is unsigned and Windows may show a security warning. There is no installer or automatic updater: close the app from its tray menu and extract a new release into a new folder to update. No offline walkthrough is included in the latest build.

What does it see, and how do I stop it?+

Your task prompt is sent for cloud planning. After capture permission, live screenshots of the selected window are sent for guidance; voice transcription also uses the cloud. Privacy masks are available in the manual source flow. The app is designed not to persist raw learner screenshots or audio. Stop is available on the companion, in the tray, and through Ctrl+Alt+Shift+S when registered. Stop cancels locally and clears permissions. Resuming needs fresh consent. This website never captures your desktop.

Can it run a scheduled task while I’m away?+

The scheduling example is about helping you create an event in a calendar app. The pilot does not include an unattended background scheduler. Phone control requires an awake, unlocked PC and an enabled desktop connection; sensitive steps still need you.
